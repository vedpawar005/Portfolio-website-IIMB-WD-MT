## Improvements made

* Moved all styling into one unified external stylesheet, `styles.css`, linked in `index.html`. Colours, fonts, layout widths, border radii, and transitions are stored as CSS custom properties (`:root` variables) at the top of the file so the entire theme can be updated from one place.
* **Fonts:** Used a five-font system imported from Google Fonts so different types of text have distinct visual roles—*Bricolage Grotesque* for main headings and buttons, *Fraunces* (soft wonky italic serif) for highlighted `<em>` words and pull-quotes, *Caveat* (handwritten script) for the tilted photo badge and side notes, *Space Mono* for dates, numbered labels, and skill tags, and *DM Sans* for clean, readable body copy.
* **Colours:** Built the theme around a five-colour palette—Prussian Blue (`#1d3557`), Steel Blue (`#457b9d`), Powder Blue (`#a8dadc`), Honeydew (`#f1faee`), and Imperial Red (`#e63946`)—supported by deep obsidian (`#0d1829`) and white. I also added a Light/Dark theme toggle button that swaps these CSS variables and saves the visitor's preference in `localStorage`.
* **Navigation bar:** Stays fixed at the top while scrolling (`position: sticky`) with a blurred glass background (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). It is built with Flexbox, highlights links on hover, includes a live location badge (`● MUMBAI × BANGALORE`), and collapses into a hamburger menu button on smaller screens.
* **Alternating Layout:** Every main section uses CSS Grid (`display: grid`) with an asymmetric two-column split (`0.78fr 1.22fr`). Odd-numbered sections place the sticky section heading on the left and content on the right, while even-numbered sections (`.reverse`) flip the grid (`1.22fr 0.78fr`) so the content appears on the left and the right-aligned heading sits on the right. Inside the content areas, 2-column and 4-column sub-grids are used for metrics, dual-engine cards, achievements, and skills.
* **Spacing and text:** Used a centred wrapper container (`min(1180px, calc(100% - 72px))`), generous vertical section padding (`96px`), a comfortable body line-height of `1.68`, and `clamp()` on all major headings so typography scales smoothly from mobile screens to wide desktop monitors.
* **Playful micro-interactions and hover effects:** Highlighted key words in headings using `<em>` with a wavy underline (`text-decoration-style: wavy`). Buttons, cards, and skill pills use a bouncy spring transition (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`) to lift and slightly tilt on hover, and the handwritten photo caption straightens out when you hover over the portrait.
* **Responsiveness, accessibility, and print:** Added responsive media queries at `900px` and `640px` so all alternating grids and multi-column cards stack cleanly into a single column on tablets and phones. Included a `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` query to disable animations for users who prefer reduced motion, and a `@media print` stylesheet that hides the navigation and page sections so printing produces a clean one-page A4 resume.

## CSS concepts used

* **Selectors:** Element selectors for base typography and resets (`body`, `h1`–`h3`, `p`, `ul`, `a`, `button`, `blockquote`), class selectors (`.hero`, `.section`, `.reverse`, `.timeline-item`, `.skill-card`, `.impact-stat`, `.paper`), ID selectors (`#home`, `#about`, `#experience`, `#playbook`, `#education`, `#skills`, `#offclock`, `#resume`, `#contact`), compound/descendant selectors (`.section.reverse .section-title`, `body.light .topbar`), and `:nth-child()` structural selectors (`:nth-child(odd)`, `:nth-child(even)`, `:nth-child(3n)`).
* **Pseudo-classes and pseudo-elements:** `:hover` for interactive lift, border, and colour states; `:first-child` and `:last-child` for targeted card and metadata styling; `::before` and `::after` for custom star bullets (`✦`) and box-sizing resets; and `::selection` to style highlighted text in Imperial Red (`#e63946`) and Honeydew (`#f1faee`).
* **Display and position:** `display: grid` and `display: flex` for all macro and micro layouts; `position: sticky` for the top navigation bar and section titles as you scroll through long content blocks; `position: relative` and `position: absolute` to overlap the tilted handwritten caption badge onto the hero portrait.
* **Box model and colour:** `padding`, `margin`, `border`, `border-left`, `border-top`, `border-radius`, layered offset `box-shadow` effects on buttons and the profile photo, `rgba()` and `color-mix()` for semi-transparent borders and surfaces, and `radial-gradient()` / `linear-gradient()` for subtle background depth and card surfaces.
* **Transitions and animations:** Smooth CSS `transition` rules with spring easing (`cubic-bezier`) and `transform` (`translateY`, `rotate`, `scale`) on hoverable elements, plus a `.reveal` / `.reveal.visible` fade-and-slide-up scroll animation triggered when sections enter the viewport.

## Design decisions

I wanted my portfolio to reflect who I actually am—an undergraduate combining business strategy and deal-making at IIM Bangalore with computational AI at the Institute of Science, Mumbai—rather than looking like a generic template. To achieve that, I structured the page as an alternating left-and-right editorial narrative with custom sections like *The Thesis*, *The Playbook*, and *Off the Clock*. Alternating the sticky section headings between the left and right columns keeps the visitor's eye engaged as they scroll down the page, while thin horizontal dividers and vertical red accent bars keep the hierarchy organised.

The five-colour palette (`#1d3557`, `#457b9d`, `#a8dadc`, `#f1faee`, and `#e63946`) balances executive navy tones with a bold Imperial Red accent so important numbers, dates, and call-to-action buttons stand out immediately. Combining playful fonts (*Bricolage Grotesque*, *Fraunces* italic, and *Caveat* handwriting) with technical and readable fonts (*Space Mono* and *DM Sans*) gives each layer of information its own voice while keeping the resume content easy to scan. Finally, I added lightweight JavaScript for three practical features: a persistent Light/Dark mode toggle, a mobile navigation menu toggle, and a scroll-reveal observer that fades each section in smoothly as you read.
